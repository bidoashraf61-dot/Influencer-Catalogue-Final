The short version of the rules for using HELVY Connect.

Last updated 9 October 2026

The service

HELVY Connect is HelloVoice’s portal for brands: browse vetted creators, build selections, request analyses and quotes, and follow the campaigns HelloVoice runs for you. HelloVoice is a BlueHolding company.

Your account

  • Sign in with your own work email. One person per account; invite colleagues rather than sharing yours.
  • Keep your sign-in codes to yourself. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used your account.
  • We may suspend an account that is misused, for example to copy the roster.

Confidential information

Creator details, analyses, scores and indicative prices in HELVY Connect are confidential. Use them only to plan campaigns with HelloVoice, and don’t copy, publish or share them outside your company.

Estimates, scores and AI

Match scores, ROI estimates and Helvy’s answers are guidance, not promises. They rest on benchmarks and on the data available at the time, and AI can make mistakes. Prices, availability and deliverables are only confirmed in a written quote from HelloVoice. Your account manager prepares it.

Credits

  • AI credits are for using AI features in HELVY Connect. They have no cash value and can’t be sold, transferred or refunded.
  • AI is free while you have an active campaign with HelloVoice, from its start date until 30 days after it ends.
  • HelloVoice may change how many credits a feature uses, and the credits it gives for your profile, invitations and the tour.

Campaigns

A campaign you book is governed by its own quote and contract with HelloVoice, not by these terms.

The service itself

We work to keep HELVY Connect available and accurate, but it is provided as it is and may change. HelloVoice owns the portal; creators own their content.

Contact

Questions about these terms: info@hellovoice.co.uk. See also our privacy notice.
