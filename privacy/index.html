Privacy

How HELVY Connect handles your information, in plain English.

Last updated 9 October 2026

Who runs HELVY Connect

HELVY Connect is run by HelloVoice, the film and media production company of BlueHolding, based in Al-Olaya, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. Questions about your information go to info@hellovoice.co.uk.

What we keep

  • Your account: your work email, name, company and job title. If you add them: phone number, photo, company logo, brands, industry, markets and language.
  • Your work in the portal: your selections, the creators you approve or reject and why, your briefs, saved ROI estimates, and the analyses and quotes you request.
  • Your chats with Helvy: kept so a conversation can continue. Your browser also keeps the last week of the chat.
  • Credits: what you earned and spent, and when.
  • Security records: sign-ins, the network address and browser used, and a device cookie that keeps your account signed in on your own devices.

How you sign in

No passwords. We email a one-time 6-digit code that works for 10 minutes and only once. We store it only in a scrambled form. HelloVoice never asks for your code by phone or chat. We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in; no advertising or tracking cookies.

AI and Google Gemini

Helvy, the AI shortlist and other AI features use Google Gemini. When you use them, your message is sent to Google for processing together with what is needed to answer it: your name and company, your brief, your open selection and the creator details the catalogue already shows you. Please don’t type sensitive personal or patient information into the chat. The ROI Calculator works on HelloVoice’s own server and sends nothing to Google.

Credits

AI features use credits. We keep a record of each credit added or spent, so you can see your balance and history in your profile.

Who can see it

  • The HelloVoice team, including your account manager, to run your selections, quotes and campaigns.
  • Colleagues who sign in with the same company email domain see the selections and campaigns you share as a team.
  • The services that run the portal for us: hosting (Amazon Web Services), email delivery and Google Gemini for AI.

We don’t sell your information.

How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep your information while your account is open. You can download your data or delete your account from your profile. Deleting it erases your personal details, chats and sign-in codes; selections and quote requests stay as business records without your details. You choose what the notification bell shows. To correct anything, contact info@hellovoice.co.uk.

Changes

If this notice changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
